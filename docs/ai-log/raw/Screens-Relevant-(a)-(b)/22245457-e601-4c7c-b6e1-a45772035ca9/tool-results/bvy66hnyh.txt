   180	### Chat 3, continued: the module and the system changes
   181	
   182	**The module**
   183	- **Conventions:** `today` is always passed in, unknown values are `null` and never 0, and dates stay strings. Every state is reproducible, and no screen does date maths.
   184	- **Default on day 30 (27 Aug),** because "30+ days" read literally includes day 30.
   185	- **Collection only when salary lands, never from other incoming money.** That's what the user agrees to. Collecting from any money drifts towards sweeping funds.
   186	- **Reminders:**
   187	  - before the due date: 25 Jul at 10:00,
   188	  - the not-collected notice: 6pm, when the attempt fails. 10:00 would go out before the attempt,
   189	  - late reminders every 7 days (a placeholder cadence),
   190	  - at most 2 in any 7 days in a row.
   191	- **The notice before default (20 Aug) is never paused,** even after a payroll delay is reported, because a material consequence must always be announced. Whether a delay should move the default date is a question, not a rule.
   192	- **`acceptTerms` is idempotent:** accepting the same terms twice returns the same advance, never a second one.
   193	- **The wallet shows the real balance,** with "Includes Rs 10,000 from your advance" on home after accepting only. On later days the balance is `null`, because spending isn't modelled. Elsewhere the advance card says "Received Rs 10,000 on 7 Jul · Rs 10,300 to repay on 28 Jul".
   194	- **The late state (S12) is viewed on 2 Aug** (5 days late).
   195	
   196	**The system**
   197	- **The frozen copy is allow-listed rather than skipped by the check,** so the exception stays narrow and visible.
   198	- **`formatAmount` in AmountText is the single money formatter,** so money in strings and in components always matches.
   199	- **Card tone borders:** attention-100 for attention, and neutral-200 for danger. Both are as faint against the page as the default edge (1.17 and 1.18:1). Full danger red would be an alarm line.
   200	- **Panel is a deprecated alias of Card,** so imports keep working and the duplicate is gone.
   201	- **The desktop scrollbar is hidden inside the frame,** because phones overlay theirs.
   202	
   203	**Calls the AI made during the build, flagged and not overruled**

   328	
   329	- Card and Panel are the same component; only the border differs.
   330	- Hard-coded colours: Card's border `#E6E6E6`, ListRow's border `#DADADA`, and `#1a1a1a` in `index.css` (duplicates neutral-900).
   331	- The secondary button's grey text (neutral-500) makes it look disabled, so the "no" option on an accept screen would look unavailable. It's also 3.95:1 on white, which fails WCAG AA at 15px. Captions and placeholders use the same grey, so the fine print on the accept screen would be the least readable text.
   332	- The only status colour is success. There's no warning, error or info, and the amber accent has no defined meaning.
   333	- Toast is success-only and has no `aria-live`. White on the success green is 3.45:1, which fails AA. (Correction: the 2.2s dismiss is in `App.jsx:25`, not in Toast.)
   334	- TextField has no label, error state or helper text, and its border is 1.41:1, too faint to show where the input is.
   335	- BottomSheet has no dialog role, focus trap or Escape handling, and tapping the backdrop closes it. Its grab handle suggests a swipe that isn't built.
